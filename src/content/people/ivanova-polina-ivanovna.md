I think I should still be called a researcher. Not an artist, a designer, or a photographer, but someone who is still feeling out their visual perception of the world, experimenting with various images, techniques, and languages. And photography has become, for me, perhaps the most natural method for transforming my vision. It is an opportunity to pause and see extraordinary interpretations within familiar silhouettes — to discern things that often fall outside our field of vision due to the rush and pace of modern life.

I deeply value and frequently employ close-ups and lighting techniques, as they allow me to convey a completely different sense of a subject, enabling me to discover and highlight its unexpected facets. For me, photography is a story of growth, discovery, and serendipity.
