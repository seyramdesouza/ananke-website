Evangel-Leo-Ken Jnr Yorke Acquaye is a multifaceted young Ghanaian writer, activist, and aspiring attorney-dentist (yes, you read that right!). A passionate human rights advocate who holds literacy and art in the highest esteem, Leo is a writer at heart whose voice is amplified by his love for humanity, the arts, and his dream of an Africa where illiteracy is nearly nonexistent.

Through his work, he embodies the spirit of a budding changemaker. As the Founder of Dear Legachi, Leo dedicates himself to empowering the young girl child and fighting against domestic violence, child marriage, and all forms of abuse. He creatively integrates STEM, filmmaking, poetry and the arts into his mission of empowerment, restoration, and dismantling archaic cultures that suppress true humanity and kindness.

When he is not writing, Leo co-leads Rhetoric Orchestra and manages the Creatives Project Ghana community. A prolific poet with hundreds of pieces to his name, his work has been featured or is forthcoming in esteemed publications such as Isele Magazine, Nenta Literary Journal, The Shallow Tales Review and the Adinkra Poetry Prize issue, among others.

As an Ambassador for Youths and Rights International and an advocate for Read Ghana, Leo's passion for social justice and education shines brightly. A self-proclaimed feminist, he is unafraid to challenge the status quo. He firmly believes that art is essential and that writing provides stability in chaos.

Leo is a writer, a fighter, and a lover.
