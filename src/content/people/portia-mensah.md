My name is Portia Mensah, and I am a Clinical Psychology intern with a deep passion for understanding people and promoting mental wellness. Outside of my professional life, I find comfort and expression in reading, writing poetry, and cooking. I call them "little pockets of joy". It helps me unwind and reconnect with myself after helping others navigate their mental health journeys.

Poetry, for me, is more than art; it is a space where emotions breathe freely and stories find meaning. Through both psychology and poetry, I hope to inspire healing and self-expression while continuing to grow into the best version of myself.
