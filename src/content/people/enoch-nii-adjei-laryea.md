Laryea Enoch Nii Adjei, also known as Sage (1992), is a multi-disciplinary artist based in Accra, Ghana. He earned a Higher National Diploma in Industrial Arts from the School of Engineering at Tamale Technical University.

His work includes painting, ceramics, illustration, graffiti, murals, and sculpture. Using these media, he engages audiences in an open dialogue on a variety of discursive cultural themes. Nii Adjei works with a variety of traditional media such as pencil, ink, pastel, charcoal, chalk, acrylic and oil paint, as well as clay, cement, fiberglass, and body painting.
