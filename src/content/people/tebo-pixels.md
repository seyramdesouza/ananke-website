Tebo Pixels is a Ghanaian visual storyteller specializing in professional photography, videography, creative media production and creative directing. Beginning with a passion for capturing authentic moments and compelling narratives, Tebo Pixels has built a reputation for delivering high-quality visual content that connects people, brands, and communities.

Driven by the philosophy that every moment deserves to be created, remembered and kept beautifully, Tebo Pixels continues to collaborate with businesses, organizations, public figures, and individuals to produce impactful visual experiences. Tebo Pixels also created the cover image for Interstice, Issue I.
