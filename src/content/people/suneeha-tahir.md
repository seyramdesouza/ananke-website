Suneeha Tahir is a designer and artist from Hyderabad, Pakistan. Her work blends digital and hand-drawn approaches, often leaning into expressive lines and playful imperfections. She has exhibited and been published internationally, including at the International Poster Biennale in Lublin. In 2024, she presented her first fine art work in a group show at VM Art Gallery, Karachi. She is currently studying Arts and Culture Management in St. Petersburg, while continuing to explore her practice and place within the arts.
