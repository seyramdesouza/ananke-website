K. Daki is the name I prefer to use on stage. I am a writer drawn to the intersections of image, thought, and experience where ordinary moments begin to carry meanings beyond themselves. My work moves across poetry and reflective prose, exploring identity, perception, relationships, and the subtle structures that shape how we understand the world.

I often write through imagery and voice, allowing objects, spaces, and ideas to speak, shift, and sometimes contradict themselves. My pieces sit in the tension between clarity and uncertainty, observation and questioning, silence and expression. At its core, my writing is an ongoing attempt to notice more closely and to trace how meaning is formed, fractured, and reimagined in everyday life.
