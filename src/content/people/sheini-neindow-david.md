My name is Sheini Neindow David, I am a visual artist from Tamale, Ghana, whose practice explores the archival recordings through photographs taken as part of my family's heritage and the life that exists within them. My work reflects on themes of memory, transformation, history, legacy and cultural identity, often finding ways in which all these stated facts reflect the connections between 'then and now' – contemporaneity.

Working across drawing – but exploring other aspects like painting, ceramics and mixed media, I engage processes that allow materials to speak about time, use, and human interaction especially personal family experiences. I am interested in the relationship between modernity and contemporaneity.

My work is meant to achieve that objective of reconstructing, bringing back to life that living memory and history that always existed in these photographs that find another home to itself on paper.
