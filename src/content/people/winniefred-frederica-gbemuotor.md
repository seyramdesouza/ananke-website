Winniefred Frederica Gbemuotor is a Ghanaian poet, communication professional, and graphic designer. Her work engages abstract ideas and embraces ambiguity, often exploring memory, grief, and love through layered, shifting perspectives.

She has contributed to national and international conversations as a discussant, including at UNESCO's Transforming Education Summit and LifeLink Tertiary Model UN. She currently serves as Social Media Management Assistant at UniMAC, where she works across content creation, photography, and videography. She is guided by the belief that small details shape larger meanings, a philosophy that informs both her creative and professional work.
