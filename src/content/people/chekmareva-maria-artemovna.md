I was born in Lisichansk, Luhansk Oblast. In early 2014, a lot began to change: my parents and I moved from one city to another, then returned home. In 2023, I moved to Crimea. Wars, deaths, and a brief stint at gunpoint became additional factors that prompted me to realize my long-held dream of becoming a photographer. I am currently based in St. Petersburg and continue to assemble a mini-collection of photographs. Most of the photographs are characterized by a disturbing element, but there are a few exceptions.
